Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Short version: Nabbit doesn't collect any data. Your reminders stay on your devices and are never sent to us or anyone else.

What Nabbit stores

Your reminders, the verbs and things you add, and your settings (theme, tone, default times, and nudge timing) are stored only on your iPhone. If you use the Apple Watch app, your iPhone sends a copy of your list to your watch directly. Nothing goes through a server, because Nabbit doesn't have one.

What Nabbit collects

Nothing. There's no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data. We can't see your reminders, and we don't know who uses Nabbit.

The microphone and speech

When you tap the mic, Nabbit listens and turns your words into a reminder using speech recognition on your iPhone. Audio and transcripts aren't saved or sent anywhere. Nabbit only uses the microphone while the mic button is listening.

Siri, widgets, and alerts

Siri, Shortcuts, widgets, Control Center, notifications, and alarms are features of iOS and watchOS. Nabbit gives them only what they need to show or change your reminders, on your devices. When you use Siri, Apple handles your request under Apple's privacy policy.

Age range for tones

The Salty and Sweary tones are for people 16+ and 18+. When you pick one, Nabbit asks Apple's age-range sharing whether you're over that age. If you choose to share, Nabbit learns only the range, not your birthday, and keeps that answer on your iPhone. If you don't share, those tones stay locked and everything else works the same.

Deleting your data

Remove a reminder by pressing and holding it, then tapping Remove. Deleting the Nabbit app deletes everything it stored on that device. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or your computer, your backup may include Nabbit's data, the same as any other app, and that backup is under your control.

Children

Nabbit doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Nabbit will never start collecting your data without saying so clearly first.
